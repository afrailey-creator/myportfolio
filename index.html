In Progress

RSL 6U CubeSat Biological Payload Program

Electrical & Computer Engineering Subteam · SCU Bronco Robotics Development Team

Designing the power and communications subsystems for a 6U CubeSat that will carry a biological payload into low Earth orbit to study bacterial growth in microgravity for vaccine research. My work focuses on the subsystem architecture decisions the rest of the spacecraft depends on.

  • Evaluating power and communications architecture against the mass, volume, and power limits of a 6U platform and the needs of the biological payload
  • Running trade studies on space-grade battery chemistries and solar panel technologies, comparing energy density, thermal performance, reliability, and flight heritage
  • Assessing communications protocols, including LoRa and radio beacons, for downlink reliability, power draw, and ground-station compatibility
  • Benchmarking against past capstone designs and published CubeSat missions to define subsystem requirements for hardware selection and fabrication
  • Earning SCU MakerLab, Robotic Systems Lab, and Dry Lab safety certifications to support hands-on CubeSat fabrication and testing
  • Power Systems
  • Energy Storage
  • Solar Power
  • RF Communications (LoRa)
  • Space Systems

FPGA Traffic Metering Controller

Designed and implemented a three-lane traffic metering controller using Verilog HDL and an Intel DE2-115 FPGA. Developed and simplified combinational logic using truth tables and Karnaugh maps, verified the design through Quartus simulation, and implemented the system on physical FPGA hardware.

  • Verilog HDL
  • Quartus Prime
  • Intel DE2-115 FPGA
  • Digital Logic
  • Karnaugh Maps

Vending Machine FSM Controller

Designed and verified a Moore finite state machine controller for a coin-operated vending system in Verilog, implementing the same design using two independent approaches: minimized Boolean equations and behavioral case-statement logic. Confirmed both implementations produced identical results across all test scenarios through RTL simulation.

  • Verilog HDL
  • Finite State Machines
  • Boolean Logic
  • RTL Simulation
  • Digital Logic
