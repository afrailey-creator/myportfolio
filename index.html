RSL 6U CubeSat Biological Payload Program

Electrical & Computer Engineering Subteam · SCU Bronco Robotics Development Team

Researching the bus communications system (radio and antenna) for a 6U CubeSat that will carry a biological payload into low Earth orbit to study bacterial growth in microgravity for vaccine research. The project is in the research and evaluation phase, and no final radio or antenna has been selected.

  • Evaluated OpenLST, an open-source UHF radio based on Planet's design used on over 200 satellites, as a candidate; reviewed its bill of materials and found two discontinued parts (RF amplifier and SAW filter) that block building it as designed, then identified a candidate replacement filter
  • Built a single-board prototype cost estimate of about $85–90 in parts, with sourcing links and plain-language notes so non-electrical teammates could follow it
  • Clarified the planned operating frequency (about 437 MHz in the 435–438 MHz amateur-satellite band) and flagged a candidate 868/915 MHz LoRa module as outside that band
  • Researched deployable UHF antennas and gave the Mechanical team envelope estimates (about 17 cm elements, 98 × 98 mm stowed module, 85–115 g), recommending an off-the-shelf module with a built-in deployer as the lower-complexity option
  • Recommended bench testing with a 70 cm-band antenna or dummy load rather than a 2.4 GHz antenna, to avoid damaging the radio's amplifier
  • Built a team reference sheet on the comms system and organized open questions for our faculty advisor on ground station access, licensing, and frequency coordination
  • Compared battery chemistries and solar panel options for the power subsystem against 6U platform limits and past CubeSat missions
  • UHF Radio

FPGA Traffic Metering Controller

Designed and implemented a three-lane traffic metering controller using Verilog HDL and an Intel DE2-115 FPGA. Developed and simplified combinational logic using truth tables and Karnaugh maps, verified the design through Quartus simulation, and implemented the system on physical FPGA hardware.

  • Verilog HDL
  • Quartus Prime
  • Intel DE2-115 FPGA
  • Digital Logic
  • Karnaugh Maps

Vending Machine FSM Controller

Designed and verified a Moore finite state machine controller for a coin-operated vending system in Verilog, implementing the same design using two independent approaches: minimized Boolean equations and behavioral case-statement logic. Confirmed both implementations produced identical results across all test scenarios through RTL simulation.

  • Verilog HDL
  • Finite State Machines
  • Boolean Logic
  • RTL Simulation
  • Digital Logic
